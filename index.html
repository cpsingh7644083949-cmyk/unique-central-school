About Our School

Unique Central School is an affiliated unit of Deshraran Singh Educational and Social Trust, located in Kailuchak, Masaurhi, Patna. Our motto, “श्रमेव जयते”, tells students that hard work leads to success.

We celebrate Independence Day and Republic Day every year, plant trees with our students, and teach children to respect the Constitution, the national heroes and each other.

Our Vision

Confident, honest and capable students who serve their community.

Our Mission

Quality education, good values and equal chances for every child.

Director’s Message

Every child is born with a spark. Our work is to protect it, feed it and help it grow. At Unique Central School we want our students to be hard-working, honest and proud of their country. I thank the parents for their trust and the teachers for their effort.

– Director

Principal’s Message

Marks are important, but character matters more. We guide each child with discipline, kindness and regular practice. Come to school on time, finish your work and respect your teachers. Parents, please stay in touch with us, because a child grows best when home and school walk together.

– Principal
